Heron Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Heron ("we") is an app that helps you spend less time on your phone. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. The short version: your Screen Time and app usage never leave your iPhone, an account is optional, and we never sell or share your data for advertising.

Who is responsible

Axel Henriksen (axelhenriksen00@icloud.com) is the controller of the personal data described here.

What stays on your iPhone

Heron uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity). The apps you pick, the apps you use, how long and when, your Rules and your blocking history are processed on your device by iOS and by Heron's extensions. We cannot see them, and they are not sent to us. Apple only gives Heron opaque tokens for the apps you choose, not their names.

Legal bases (EEA and UK)

  • Contract: running the account, sync, support and Pro features you ask for.
  • Consent: notifications and product events. You can withdraw consent at any time in iOS Settings or in Heron's Settings.
  • Legitimate interests: keeping Heron secure and preventing abuse, for example the 24-hour waiting time before an account deleted on the same email or Apple ID can be created again.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase (database, sign-in and server functions), hosted in the European Union.
  • Apple (Sign in with Apple, push notifications and App Store payments), under Apple's own privacy policy.

We do not sell personal data, and we share it with no one else unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Account data: until you delete your account.
  • Support messages and feedback: until you delete your account, or earlier if you ask. Feedback sent while signed out is not linked to you.
  • Product events: 180 days, then they are deleted automatically.
  • Averages shown in the app (such as the typical weekly Focus Hours of members with your goal) are computed from at least 20 members and never show anyone's own data.
  • When you delete your account we keep a one-way hash of your email or Apple ID identifier for about a week, only to enforce the 24-hour re-signup rule. It cannot be turned back into the email.

Your rights

You can see and correct your data in the app. You can anonymise your account (it clears your name and answers but keeps your purchases) or delete it with all data linked to it under Settings → My Account. You can also ask us for a copy of your data, to correct or erase it, or to restrict or object to processing by writing to axelhenriksen00@icloud.com. In the EEA you may complain to your data protection authority (in Denmark, Datatilsynet).

Children

Heron is not directed at children under 13. If you enter an age under 13, it stays on your iPhone and is not stored on our servers. Parents can contact us to have a child's account deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
